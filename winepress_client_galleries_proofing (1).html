How to Link Google Drive to Winepress

Mandatory Step 1: Set Folder Permissions
In Google Drive, right-click your client photo folder → Share → change General Access to "Anyone with the link" (Viewer). This allows your clients' browsers to display the photos cleanly.

Method A: Zero-API-Key Multi-Link Paste (Fastest — 10 Seconds)

  1. Open your shared Google Drive folder and select your photos (Ctrl+A or Cmd+A).
  2. Click the Copy Link icon in the Google Drive toolbar (or right-click → Copy links).
  3. Paste them into Option B: Zero-API-Key Drive Link Importer in Studio Admin and click Convert & Add Drive Photos.

Method B: 1-Click Entire Folder Auto-Sync (Preserves Original Camera Filenames)

  1. Go to Google Cloud Console, enable Google Drive API, and click Create Credentials → API Key (100% free).
  2. Paste your API Key once into the Google Drive API Key box in Studio Admin (it saves to your studio cloud automatically).
  3. For every future client shoot, just paste the Google Drive Folder Link and click Fetch & Sync Drive Folder—it pulls every JPEG/PNG and keeps exact camera filenames (`WP_0042.JPG`) for Lightroom filtering!
